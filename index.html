Story Maker

A personal desktop app that helps one creator turn text stories into short vertical videos and publish them to their own TikTok account.

What it does

Story Maker runs on the creator's own computer. It takes a written story, narrates it with a synthetic (AI-generated) voice, adds word-by-word captions and a title card over gameplay footage, and renders a vertical video. The stories come from posts that are publicly available on Reddit; the app shows which subreddit a story came from.

How it uses TikTok

  • Login Kit — the creator connects their own TikTok account. The app shows the account's name and picture so they can see which account is connected.
  • Content Posting API (Direct Post) — the creator reviews a finished video in the app, edits the caption and hashtags, chooses who can view it, and confirms. Only then is the video sent to TikTok. Nothing is ever posted automatically or on a schedule.
  • Content Posting API (Upload) — as an alternative the creator can send a video to their TikTok inbox as a draft and finish the post in the TikTok app.

Videos are labelled as AI-generated content because the narration is synthetic. Promotional-content disclosure, privacy level and interaction settings are always chosen by the creator for each post.

Your data

Everything stays on the creator's computer. See the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.
