Privacy Policy

Story Maker · Last updated: 29 September 2026

Story Maker is a personal desktop app that turns text stories into short videos and, if you connect a TikTok account, lets you publish them to that account.

What the app accesses

When you connect TikTok, the app receives through TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API:

  • your TikTok display name, username and profile picture (scope user.info.basic and the creator information the Content Posting API returns), shown in the app so you can see which account is connected and which privacy options TikTok offers you;
  • an access token and a refresh token that let the app upload videos to your TikTok inbox (scope video.upload) and post videos you have confirmed to your account (scope video.publish).

The app does not read your TikTok videos, followers, messages, or any other account data.

Where data is stored

All data stays on the computer running the app. Tokens and upload history are saved in local files in the app's data folder. The developer does not receive, collect or store any of it. Nothing is sent to any server other than TikTok's and Reddit's official interfaces, and nothing is sold or shared with third parties. The app contains no analytics or advertising.

Removing your data

Click “Disconnect” on the app's TikTok page. This revokes the token with TikTok and deletes it from the computer. You can also remove the app's access in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services.
